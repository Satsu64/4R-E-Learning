What is Source Reduction?

Choosing inputs wisely to avoid generating solid waste before, during, and after product use. Preventing waste eliminates collection, fuel, and processing costs entirely.

Core Principles (SWCorp Module)

Plan purchases & make strict grocery lists
Pick items with minimal or biodegradable packaging
Use rechargeable batteries over disposable ones
Bring personal food containers (Say NO to Plastics)

School & Home Actions

  • Double-sided printing on paper
  • Campaign: 'Value Food, Avoid Waste'
  • Say NO to single-use polystyrene and plastic straws
5 Ways Recognized Reuse Approaches (SWCorp)
Upcycling Adds aesthetic & functional market value

The 5 Modalities of Reuse (SWCorp Guidelines)

1
Conventional Reuse: Using containers for their original purpose repeatedly.
2
Creative Reuse (Upcycling): Transforming scrap into higher products.
3
Reclamation: Salvaging building timbers for furniture.
4
Refurbishing / Repair: Mending electronics and appliances.

Adaptive Reuse

Modifying old structures into community centers.
