Start Small, Think Big

Remember, every small action counts! You don't have to implement all these tips at once. Start with a few that work best for your lifestyle and gradually incorporate more. Together, we can make a significant positive impact on our planet.

Daily Sustainability Tips

Practical tips to make sustainable living easy and effective

  • 1 Turn off lights, fans, and appliances when leaving a room.
  • 2 Unplug chargers and electronics to prevent standby phantom power loss.
  • 3 Install water-saving aerators on kitchen and bathroom taps.
  • 4 Switch conventional incandescent bulbs to energy-efficient LEDs.
  • 1 Bring durable canvas tote bags whenever you go grocery shopping.
  • 2 Buy pantry staples in bulk to minimize single-use packaging.
  • 3 Choose products manufactured with certified post-consumer recycled content.
  • 4 Avoid individually wrapped single-serve snacks and produce.
  • 1 Plan weekly meals in advance to prevent perishable ingredients from spoiling.
  • 2 Compost fruit rinds, vegetable peels, and coffee grounds at home.
  • 3 Store food in clear airtight glass containers to keep items fresh longer.
  • 4 Repurpose vegetable scraps into homemade cooking broth before discarding.
  • 1 Wash garments in cold water and air-dry them on laundry racks.
  • 2 Mend loose buttons and repair torn seams instead of throwing clothes out.
  • 3 Donate gently-worn clothing to local charitable shelters or thrift stores.
  • 4 Choose timeless, durable natural fabrics over fast-fashion synthetic blends.
  • 1 Carpool or share rides with colleagues, neighbors, and friends.
  • 2 Use public transit buses and commuter trains for regular daily commutes.
  • 3 Walk or cycle for short neighborhood errands to cut carbon emissions.
  • 4 Maintain proper tire pressure to improve vehicle fuel economy.
